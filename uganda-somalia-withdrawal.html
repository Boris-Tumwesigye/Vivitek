UPDF Begins Somalia Withdrawal as Uganda Sets 2027 Exit Deadline

Published: 8 November 2026 | By Vivitek News Desk

Uganda has begun withdrawing some of its troops from Somalia, marking the start of a phased military exit after nearly two decades of involvement in the country.

The Uganda People's Defence Forces (UPDF) announced on 2 November 2026 that the first group of soldiers deployed under bilateral arrangements with Somalia's Federal Government had already returned to Uganda.

In a statement attributed to Acting Director of Defence Public Information Colonel Chris Magezi, the military said the withdrawal of troops serving under the bilateral arrangement would be completed within the following days.

Not all Ugandan troops have left Somalia

The initial withdrawal concerns troops deployed under a separate bilateral arrangement. It does not mean that every Ugandan soldier has already left Somalia.

Ugandan personnel also serve under the African Union Support and Stabilisation Mission in Somalia (AUSSOM), which supports Somalia's security operations against armed groups, including al-Shabaab.

Uganda has announced that it intends to withdraw all its remaining forces, including those serving under AUSSOM, by the end of 2027.

Nearly two decades of military involvement

Uganda first deployed troops to Somalia in 2007 under the African Union Mission in Somalia (AMISOM). The mission later transitioned through different arrangements before becoming AUSSOM.

Ugandan forces have played a major role in supporting Somalia's government and security forces in the fight against al-Shabaab, an armed group that continues to carry out attacks in the country.

Security questions as withdrawal progresses

The planned departure raises questions about how Somalia will maintain security as foreign forces reduce their presence.

Reporting by the Associated Press has highlighted concerns about the security responsibilities that Somali forces may need to assume as Ugandan troops withdraw from positions in the country.

The transition will therefore require coordination between Somali authorities, the African Union and other international partners to avoid security gaps.

What happens next?

The withdrawal is expected to take place in phases. The end-of-2027 deadline announced by Uganda covers its remaining forces in Somalia, including those serving under the African Union mission.

The actual progress of the withdrawal and the security situation will remain important developments to follow in the coming months.
