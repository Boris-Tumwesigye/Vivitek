5 Simple Ways to Stay Healthy Every Day

Small everyday habits can help support your health and wellbeing.

1. Eat a balanced diet

Include a variety of vegetables, fruits, whole grains and protein sources in your meals. Choose foods that suit your needs and are available locally.

2. Drink enough safe water

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Use water that is safe to drink, and take extra care in hot weather or when you are physically active.

3. Stay physically active

Walking, dancing, gardening and other activities can help you move more. Start at a level that feels comfortable and increase gradually.

4. Get enough sleep

A regular sleep schedule and a comfortable, quiet sleeping environment can support rest and daily functioning.

5. Look after your mental wellbeing

Make time to relax, connect with people you trust and take breaks when possible. Seek professional support if emotional difficulties persist or interfere with daily life.

A final reminder

Healthy habits are not a substitute for medical care. If you have symptoms or concerns about your health, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
