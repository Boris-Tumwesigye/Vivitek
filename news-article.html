Uganda Introduces Three New Purple-Fleshed Sweet Potato Varieties

Uganda has unveiled three improved purple-fleshed sweet potato varieties in an initiative aimed at supporting nutrition, food security and agricultural development.

What happened?

According to Uganda Broadcasting Corporation (UBC), the varieties were unveiled in Mbarara during activities marking World Food Day 2026. The initiative highlights the role that improved crops can play in strengthening food systems and supporting farming communities.

Why does it matter?

Purple-fleshed sweet potatoes are of interest because of their nutritional qualities. Improved varieties may also create opportunities for farmers and contribute to household food supplies. Their actual benefits will depend on factors such as availability, farming conditions and how they are used.

What happens next?

Farmers, agricultural organisations and communities will be important in determining how widely the new varieties are adopted and how they contribute to local food security.
